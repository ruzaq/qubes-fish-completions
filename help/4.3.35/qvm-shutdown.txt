usage: qvm-shutdown [--verbose] [--quiet] [--help] [--version] [--all]
                    [--exclude EXCLUDE] [--wait] [--timeout TIMEOUT] [--force]
                    [--dry-run]
                    [VMNAME ...]

Shutdown a qube

positional arguments:
  VMNAME             zero or more domain names

options:
  --verbose, -v      increase verbosity
  --quiet, -q        decrease verbosity
  --help, -h         show this help message and exit
  --version          show program's version number and exit
  --all              perform the action on all qubes
  --exclude EXCLUDE  exclude the qube from --all
  --wait             wait for the VMs to shut down
  --timeout TIMEOUT  timeout after which domains are killed when using --wait
                     (default: 60)
  --force            shut down even if other qubes depend on this one (e.g. as
                     NetVM or AudioVM); does not affect how the qube itself is
                     shut down; use with caution
  --dry-run          don't really shutdown or kill the domains; useful with
                     --wait
